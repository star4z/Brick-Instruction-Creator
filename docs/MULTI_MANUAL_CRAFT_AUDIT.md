## 1. 3D Rendering, Edge Lines, Materials & Lighting (16 Findings)

1. **[New] Thin 1px High-Contrast Red New-Part Silhouette Outlines on Dark Themes** (`10278` p. 132, 214, 286): On pitch-black and dark-canvas manuals, newly placed plates/tiles receive a razor-thin (`~1px`) crimson perimeter outline around only their exposed exterior edges — making dark-bluish-gray tiles placed onto a dark-bluish-gray 32×32 baseplate instantaneously legible without heavy cartoon borders.
2. **[New] Soft Contact Ambient Occlusion (AO) in Stud Crevices & Interior Rooms** (`10278` p. 64, 95, 174; `42083` p. 62): Subtle screen-space or baked crevice darkening where bricks meet baseplates, recessed windows, and dense Technic pin clusters dramatically improves depth perception compared to flat ambient shading.
3. **[Polish] Adaptive Specular Highlight on Glossy vs. Matte Plastics** (`75895` p. 34, 55; `10281` p. 47): Smooth curved slopes, windscreens, and metallic/chrome rims show crisp tight specular highlights while frosted/rubber tires and matte Technic pins stay diffuse.
4. **[New] Translucent / Clear Glass Fresnel Rim & Interior Refraction Hint** (`10278` p. 174, 285; `75895` p. 55): Transparent windscreens and window panes render with subtle bluish-white edge Fresnel reflections and faint internal visibility so they never disappear against dark or light backgrounds.
5. **[New] Rubber Tire & Flexible Hose Matte Dark-Charcoal Material Separation** (`75895` p. 73; `42083` p. 62): Rubber elements (tires, treads, rubber bands) use distinct matte low-specular shading with softer gray edge lines (`#64748b`) rather than sharp plastic bevel edges.
6. **[New] Metallic / Pearl / Drum-Lacquered Shader Distinction** (`42083` p. 170; `92176` p. 186): Metallic Silver, Pearl Gold, and Flat Silver parts benefit from a subtle dual-lobe metallic sheen so builders never confuse Pearl Gold with Warm Tan or Metallic Silver with Light Bluish Gray.
7. **[Polish] Stud-Top Rim Highlighting on Dark Baseplates** (`10278` p. 8, 19): On large dark baseplates (32×32+), circular stud rims catch a crisp light top-left crescent so builders can accurately count stud coordinates (`3 studs from edge`) without straining.
8. **[New] Underside Anti-Stud Tube & Hollow-Stud Interior Shading** (`10278` p. 214 Step 301): When a step flips upside-down to attach plates underneath, inner anti-stud tubes and ribs need crisp interior edge contrast so underside stud-grid alignment is unmistakable.
9. **[New] Background-Aware Part Drop Shadow Suppression on Pitch-Black Themes** (`10278` p. 8–286; `31199` p. 24–93): On pure `#000000` canvases, drop shadows are suppressed and replaced with crisp white/silver edge rims (`#e2e8f0`) on dark bricks so outer silhouettes never melt into the page.
10. **[New] White-Halo Outer Rim Option for Pitch-Black Manuals** (`10278` p. 8, 132): Black bricks on black backgrounds (`#000000`) use brighter silver-white (`#e5e7eb`) outer crease lines than on light-gray manuals (`#cbd5e1`).
11. **[New] Previous-Step Subtle Desaturation / Contrast Separation Mode** (`31172` p. 47; `10278` p. 64): Rather than washing out previous steps with milky white opacity (which looks muddy on dark themes), allow an optional subtle luminance/saturation step-down (`fadeMode: 'tint' | 'desaturate' | 'outline-only'`).
12. **[New] High-DPI Vector-Crisp Stud-Count Grid Overlay Toggle** (`10278` p. 8, 19; `92176` p. 61): For long plates/beams (`>= 8` studs), optional faint stud-counting tick marks every 4 studs during high-density placement steps prevent off-by-one placement errors.
13. **[Polish] Consistent Key-Light Azimuth Relative to Camera** (`42083` p. 62, 93; `10278` p. 174): Ensure the primary directional light rotates smoothly with the camera (always ~35° upper-left of viewer) so rotated/flipped views never throw the front face into flat shadow.
14. **[New] Printed Graphic & Decal Crispness Boost (Anisotropic / Unfiltered UV)** (`92176` p. 85, 150; `75895` p. 34): Printed tiles (e.g., `USA` flags, `Turbo` badges, `IRON MAN` tiles) should bypass low-pass softening and preserve sharp text/artwork legibility at all thumbnail scales.
15. **[New] Subtle Ground-Plane Contact Shadow for Floating Subassemblies** (`10281` p. 36; `75895` p. 10): Standalone subassemblies floating on light-gray/blue canvases look grounded when given an ultra-subtle directional floor shadow directly beneath their lowest Y plane.
16. **[New] Color-Accurate Neutral White Balance on Gray Canvases** (`42083` p. 37–278; `10281` p. 11–76): White bricks on light-gray canvases (`#e3e4e5`) require clean `#1f2937` crease lines and slightly warm-neutral shading so they read as bright white without blowing out highlights.

## 2. Placement Arrows, Motion Glyphs & Insertion Physics (16 Findings)

17. **[New] Segmented Dashed-Tail Motion Arrows for Slide/Push Insertions** (`42083` p. 42 Step 40, p. 247 Step 397): Long sliding insertions (axles pushed through shock absorbers, rods through frames) use segmented dashed-tail red arrows (`▪ ▪ ▸`) that visually communicate "slide smoothly through" vs. solid drop arrows.
18. **[New] Red Circular Target Highlight Ring (`○`) on Dense Assemblies** (`42083` p. 247 Step 397, p. 278 Step 449): When attaching a small subassembly or pin onto a huge, visually dense chassis, draw a crisp red circular ring directly around the exact destination pin/hole on the main assembly so the eye locks onto the attachment point immediately.
19. **[New] Zoomed-In Destination Magnifier Callout Bubble** (`42083` p. 210 Step 334): When a single tiny pin/bush is inserted deep inside a complex chassis, render a small square/circular zoomed-in close-up callout bubble connected by a thin leader line directly to the destination hole.
20. **[New] In-Scene Floating Length Badges Next to Inserted Axles/Beams** (`42083` p. 37 Step 28, p. 42 Step 40, p. 74 Step 89 & 91, p. 130 Step 191, p. 148 Step 217): When a step inserts axles or beams of different lengths (e.g., both a `4L` and an `8L` axle in Step 91), float the circled `(4)` / `(8)` or squared `[5]` badge directly beside each part in the 3D scene so the builder never swaps them.
21. **[New] Curved Yellow Simultaneous-Fold / Articulation Multi-Arrows** (`92176` p. 186 Step 325; `42083` p. 247): When 2–4 symmetric hinged arms/legs/flaps fold simultaneously in a single step, render synchronized curved yellow motion arrows on each articulated limb.
22. **[New] Curved Yellow "Repeat Around 360°" Cylindrical Arrow** (`92176` p. 150 Step 241): When a `4×` or `6×` subassembly wraps symmetrically around a cylindrical core, pair the callout leader with a curved yellow orbital arrow around the cylinder axis indicating "repeat evenly around all sides".
23. **[New] Chunky Blue Swap / Interchange Arrow for Alternate Configurations** (`10281` p. 76; `75895` p. 4): Alternate-build swaps (green vs. pink bonsai foliage; helmet vs. hair on minifigure) use a distinct double-ended or segmented blue/yellow interchange arrow rather than red assembly arrows.
24. **[New] Multi-Part Synchronized Drop-Column Arrows (Simultaneous Parallel Drops)** (`75895` p. 25 Step 23; `10278` p. 38 Step 30): When two identical clips/tiles drop in parallel onto adjacent studs, align their arrow tails to the exact same horizontal datum so they read as one clean visual gesture.
25. **[New] Angled/Diagonal Insertion Axis Alignment for Non-90° Hinges & Slopes** (`10281` p. 26 Step 26; `42083` p. 42 Step 39): Parts attached onto angled hinges or ball joints should offset and draw their placement arrow strictly along the transformed local stud/pin vector (not snapped to world X/Y/Z).
26. **[New] Curved "Snap-In / Pivot-Into-Place" Arrow for Clips & Ball Joints** (`10281` p. 47; `42083` p. 170): Clip-on bars and ball-socket joints benefit from a gentle curved snap arrow showing the rotational seating motion rather than a stiff straight translation arrow.
27. **[New] Push-Until-Flush / Stop-Depth Indicator on Partial Axle Insertions** (`42083` p. 74 Step 89, p. 148 Step 217): When an axle stops flush inside a beam or protrudes by an exact stud count, show a crisp depth-stop tick or `1:1` protrusion callout.
28. **[New] Two-Stage Sequential Arrows (Insert Then Rotate/Lock)** (`42083` p. 93 Step 120): For bayonet-style locking pins or levers that insert along one axis and then pivot 90° to lock, support a primary red insertion arrow paired with a secondary yellow rotation arrow.
29. **[Polish] Arrowhead Obstacle-Clearance Auto-Retract** (`75895` p. 65 Step 4–6): Ensure arrow shafts never pierce adjacent studs or neighboring newly-placed bricks in tight 4-stud-wide Speed Champions subassemblies.
30. **[New] Optional Subtle White Outline Halo on Red Arrows Over Dark Bricks** (`10278` p. 38, 132): On pitch-black themes or dark brown/black subassemblies, give red placement arrows a crisp `1px` white/light outline so they pop with maximum print legibility.
31. **[New] Zero-Arrow Clarity Mode for Simple Top-Down Plate Stacking** (`10278` p. 64, 95; `10281` p. 11, 17): Official LEGO manuals intentionally omit arrows on obvious unobstructed top-down wall/plate stacking steps, relying on clean framing (or thin red outlines on dark themes) to avoid visual clutter — expose an "Official Minimal Arrows" preset.
32. **[New] Arrow-Free "Press Firmly / Seat Flush" Compression Double-Arrow Glyph** (`92176` p. 85; `31199` p. 93): When joining two large completed sub-sections (e.g., rocket stages or mosaic canvas plates), render bold opposing compression arrows indicating firm mating pressure.

## 3. Parts List Boxes (PLI), Length Badges, 1:1 Guides & Color Differentiation (16 Findings)

33. **[New] Top-of-Page Inline `1:1` Ruler Card Paired Beside the PLI Box** (`42083` p. 37 Step 28, p. 74 Step 89, p. 130 Step 191, p. 148 Step 217, p. 247 Step 397): Instead of cramming `1:1` silhouettes inside the PLI box (which bloats PLI height), render a dedicated clean white `1:1` card right beside the PLI box — capable of stacking multiple `1:1` parts (e.g., `4L` + `8L` axles together on p. 74) inside one unified strip!
34. **[New] Color-Matched `1:1` Silhouettes (Not Just Monochrome Gray)** (`42083` p. 37 yellow/black axles, p. 148 red 6L axle, p. 247 brown 5L axle): Render `1:1` axle/bar/beam silhouettes in the actual brick color (yellow, red, reddish-brown, tan, black) with crisp outline strokes so builders match both length AND color at a glance.
35. **[New] `1:1` Stud-Grid & Gear-Tooth Verification Guides** (`42083` p. 74; `75192`): Extend `1:1` guides beyond straight axles/beams to include curved/angled Technic liftarms (`3×5 L-beam`, `2×4 L-beam`), hoses/tubes (length in cm/studs), and Technic gears (with tooth count badges like `12T`, `16T`, `20T`, `24T`).
36. **[New] Pitch-Black / Dark-Theme Hairline-Bordered PLI Boxes** (`10278` p. 8–286; `31199` p. 105): On pitch-black themes, PLI boxes use a pure black (or ultra-dark `#0b0f17`) fill with a crisp `1px` white hairline border (`#ffffff`) rather than a glaring white box that breaks dark-mode cohesion.
37. **[New] Clean Neutral White PLI Boxes on Technical Gray Canvases** (`42083` p. 37–247; `10281` p. 11–68): On gray canvases (`#d9dbdd` / `#e3e4e5`), crisp white PLI cards with `1px` charcoal borders (`#475569`) create calm editorial hierarchy without heavy blue saturation.
38. **[New] Submodel Finished-Preview Header Docked Directly Above the PLI** (`42083` p. 170 Step 266; `10281` p. 48 Step 61; `92176` p. 76 Step 97; `75895` p. 6 Step 1, p. 25 Step 23, p. 73 Step 54): On the first step of a multi-step submodel or section, dock a compact rounded preview box showing the finished submodel + `2x` / `3x` / `4x` multiplier right *above* Step 1's PLI box.
39. **[New] Zero-PLI Pure-Attachment & Articulation Steps** (`10281` p. 36 Step 49; `92176` p. 85 Step 118, p. 186 Step 324; `75895` p. 17 Step 15, p. 55): When a step adds zero new loose parts (only attaching a previously built subassembly, folding hinges, or rotating a section), omit the PLI box cleanly instead of showing an empty box.
40. **[New] Consolidated Multi-Substep PLI for Entire Callout Sequences** (`42083` p. 93 Step 120, p. 170 Step 266; `10281` p. 58 Step 84; `10278` p. 253 Step 342): When a step contains a 2–7 substep callout sequence, consolidate ALL parts needed for the entire callout into the step's single top-left PLI box (multiplied by `repeatCount`, e.g., `3x` → `12x, 12x, 3x, 30x` on Bonsai p. 58).
41. **[New] Dual-Row Balanced PLI Auto-Wrapping** (`10278` p. 133 Step 175, p. 253 Step 342, p. 286 Step 391): When a step has 5–9 distinct PLI items, auto-wrap into 2 balanced rows (e.g., 3+3 or 4+3) rather than one ultra-wide strip that collides with the 3D render.
42. **[New] PLI Part Orientation Standardization (Canonical 3/4 Isometric)** (`10278` p. 132; `75895` p. 10, 73): Render every PLI thumbnail from a standardized canonical isometric angle (e.g., showing studs and distinctive side clips/holes clearly) rather than its arbitrary world-space rotation in the step.
43. **[New] Confusable Similar-Hue Pill Differentiation Beyond Dark Colors** (`10281` p. 52 light green vs. dark green; `31199` p. 35 dark red vs. brown vs. reddish-brown; `92176` p. 111 tan vs. dark tan): Generalize `detectConfusablePliColors` from dark-only colors to *any* pair of perceptually close hues in the same PLI (e.g., Tan vs. Dark Tan, Light Bluish Gray vs. White, Dark Red vs. Reddish Brown, Bright Green vs. Sand Green), displaying high-contrast color-code/name dots whenever redmean distance `< 48`.
44. **[New] PLI Quantity-First Typography Alignment** (`42083` p. 37; `10278` p. 19; `75895` p. 10): Standardize crisp `1x`, `2x`, `4x` quantity labels left-aligned directly below each thumbnail with tabular numerals so columns scan effortlessly.
45. **[New] Scatter-Quantity Callout Boxes for Repetitive Organic Decoration** (`10281` p. 52 Step 70): When a single step scatters a large count (`>= 8`) of identical small decorative elements (leaves, studs, flowers, pebbles) across many non-critical positions, support compact floating `[part + 33x] ↓` quantity callout boxes pointing into the target zone.
46. **[New] Oversized Baseplate / Frame PLI Exclusion or Corner Thumbnail Rule** (`10278` p. 8 Step 1): When Step 1 begins with a massive 32×32 or 48×48 baseplate, render the baseplate as a compact tilted isometric chip inside the PLI (or as a loose-part pre-step) so it doesn't blow up the PLI box height.
47. **[New] Minifigure Accessory Grouping Inside PLI** (`75895` p. 4; `92176` p. 186 Step 325): Group microfigure/minifigure elements (helmet + visor + hair + torso + legs) visually together in PLI order (top-to-bottom body order) for instant mental mapping.
48. **[New] Wheel + Tire + Hubcap Exploded PLI / Callout Pairing** (`75895` p. 73 Step 54): Automatically recognize concentric rim + tire + hubcap assemblies and display them in a clean concentric vertical exploded stack with `2x` / `4x` multiplier.

## 4. Subassemblies, Callout Boxes, Leaders & Repeat Multipliers (16 Findings)

49. **[New] Multi-Column Callout Box Layouts (`2×2`, `3+2`, `2+3+2`)** (`42083` p. 93 Step 120; `10281` p. 58 Step 84; `10278` p. 253 Step 342): Support 2-column and 3-column sequential sub-step grids inside a single callout box (e.g., `1 2` left column | `3 4` right column, or `1 2 3` | `4 5`) separated by clean hairline dividers — essential for 4–7 step subassemblies without creating a tall skinny strip.
50. **[New] Simultaneous Multi-Callout Micro-Assemblies on One Page** (`10278` p. 38 Step 30): Allow a single step (especially interior room furnishing or exterior greebling) to display **2–3 independent floating callout boxes** placed around the perimeter of the main model, each with its own thin orthogonal leader pointing to its exact room/stud location!
51. **[New] Horizontal Bottom-Strip 4-Step Callout Bar** (`42083` p. 247 Step 397): When a wide landscape model fills the upper 70% of the page, dock a sleek horizontal `1  2  3  4` callout strip along the bottom edge with a vertical leader rising straight to the red target ring on the chassis!
52. **[New] Clean White / Neutral-Gray Callout Boxes with Hairline Borders** (`42083` p. 37, 93, 148; `10281` p. 30, 31; `10278` p. 19, 38, 133): Offer a theme-configurable callout fill (`cream` for 2025/Creator/Ideas vs. `white` for Botanicals/Technic vs. `black-with-white-hairline` for 18+ Dark themes) so callouts harmonize with every theme preset.
53. **[New] Corner-Docked Miniature 1-Step Callout Inset** (`10281` p. 31 Step 36; `10278` p. 19 Step 12, p. 133 Step 175): For a 2-piece micro-subassembly (e.g., a 1×2 plate + clip or a 2-piece lamp), render a tiny tight-fitting square inset box right next to the target zone with a short straight arrow pointing into place — no oversized callout chrome.
54. **[New] Prominent Standalone `2x` / `3x` / `4x` Repeat Multiplier Placement** (`42083` p. 42 Step 40, p. 170 Step 266; `92176` p. 61 Step 69, p. 76 Step 100; `10281` p. 58 Step 84): Display bold `2x` / `4x` repeat multipliers cleanly at the bottom-right corner of the final substep in a repeated subassembly sequence AND in the intro preview header so builders know *before* building how many copies to make.
55. **[New] Chiral Left/Right Mirrored Side-by-Side Callout Pairing** (`75192` p. 459; `42083`): Upgrade our chiral mirror detection from a badge to an optional **side-by-side L+R mirrored callout card** sharing one consolidated `2×` PLI when both chiral halves attach in consecutive steps.
56. **[New] Subassembly Entry/Exit Visual Continuity (Previous-Step Carry-Over Arrow)** (`75895` p. 17 Step 15, p. 55; `92176` p. 85 Step 118, p. 186 Step 324): When a subassembly finished on the *previous* page is brought onto the main build on the *next* page, draw a clean horizontal carry-over leader entering from the left page margin directly to the attachment point!
57. **[New] Inline Callout Sub-Step Number Typography Hierarchy** (`42083` p. 93; `10281` p. 58; `92176` p. 41, 111, 137): Sub-step numbers (`1`, `2`, `3`) inside callouts use lighter weight and ~55–65% font size of the main step number (`120`, `84`, `223`) so builders never confuse substep `2` with Step `2`.
58. **[New] Subassembly Rotation / Orientation Consistency With Main Assembly** (`92176` p. 111 Step 157, p. 137 Step 223): Orient the final substep of an inline callout to match the *exact* azimuth/orientation in which it drops onto the main assembly — eliminating mental rotation errors.
59. **[New] Inline Callout Auto-Placement Using Empty-Quadrant Occupancy Map** (`10278` p. 19, 38, 133; `92176` p. 150): Compute the 2D screen-space bounding silhouette of the main 3D render and automatically dock inline callout boxes into the largest unoccupied corner quadrant (avoiding PLI and main model overlap).
60. **[New] Callout-to-Target Multi-Forked Leader Auto-Routing With Obstacle Avoidance** (`10278` p. 19 Step 12; `31172`): Route orthogonal leader segments cleanly around the main model silhouette (or with a crisp contrast halo where crossing over dark bricks) rather than cutting diagonally through focal details.
61. **[New] Submodel Chapter "Build N Copies" Opening Progress Pill** (`10281` p. 48; `92176` p. 76): On multi-page repeated submodels (`3x`, `4x`), show a persistent subtle `×3` / `×4` reminder badge in the corner of every step page in that submodel chapter, not just its first page.
62. **[New] Callout Collapse-to-Single-Step Heuristic for 2-Piece Trivial Subassemblies** (`10281` p. 31 Step 36): Automatically render 2-piece micro-subassemblies as a 1-frame inset (showing the assembled 2-piece unit directly) instead of a bloated 2-step callout.
63. **[New] Interactive 3D HTML Expandable Subassembly Inspection** (`42083` p. 170; `10278` p. 253): In Interactive 3D HTML export, allow clicking any inline callout box to isolate and orbit that subassembly in 3D before returning to the main build view.
64. **[New] Callout Background Contrast Guard on Dark Themes** (`10278` p. 19, 38, 133): On dark themes, ensure inline callouts with dark bricks either use a subtle elevated charcoal surface (`#141923`) or crisp white/silver edge lines so small brown/black parts inside the callout remain 100% legible.

## 5. Camera Framing, Rotation, Underside Flips & Regional Close-Ups (14 Findings)

65. **[New] 3-Axis Rotation Icon Differentiation (`Rotate Horizontal 180°` vs. `Flip Upside-Down` vs. `Tilt/Roll 90°`)** (`10278` p. 214 Step 301 & p. 215 Step 302; `42083` p. 74 Step 89, p. 278 Step 449; `75895` p. 45): Distinguish between:
    - **Yaw 180° (turntable rotate)**: dual circular arrows around vertical axis (`42083` p. 74, `92176` p. 61).
    - **Pitch 180° (flip upside-down)**: vertical flip-over double-arc icon (`10278` p. 214–215, `75895` p. 45).
    - **90° Quarter-Turn**: compact circular turn arrow with clean visual cue.
66. **[New] Explicit Vertical "Stand Upright / Lay Flat" Orientation Transition Icon** (`92176` p. 85, 111; `42083` p. 130): When a build transitions from horizontal assembly to vertical standing (or vice versa), emit a clear 90° pitch-transition badge so builders know to stand the model up.
67. **[New] Dynamic Elevation Adaptation for Tall Vertical vs. Flat Floor Steps** (`92176` p. 85, 150 vs. `10278` p. 8, 19, 133): Flat baseplate/tiling steps automatically adopt a steeper top-down isometric elevation (`~40°–48°`) for clear stud counting, while tall cylindrical/tower builds adopt a lower architectural elevation (`~20°–26°`) so vertical facades aren't foreshortened.
68. **[New] Regional Close-Up Crop With Soft-Edge Frame & Full-Model Locator Minimap** (`75192`; `42083` p. 210): Upgrade our `'regional'` framing mode so large models (`>= 120` parts) can automatically switch to regional framing when a step adds `<= 6` small parts localized to `< 20%` of the model's bounding radius, with a crisp corner locator minimap.
69. **[New] Consistent Submodel-to-Main Camera Azimuth Handoff** (`10278` p. 253 → p. 285; `92176` p. 137): When transitioning from a submodel chapter back to the main build where that submodel attaches, smoothly match or clearly annotate any camera azimuth change so the builder immediately recognizes the seam.
70. **[New] Camera Anchor Stability (Anti-Jitter Centering)** (`10278` p. 64 → 95 → 174; `10281` p. 30 → 31): On sequential steps of the same floor/submodel where bounding box grows slowly, smoothly damp camera center and zoom changes so the model doesn't "jump" erratically from step to step.
71. **[New] Interior-Room Cutaway / Wall-Visibility Camera Angle Selection** (`10278` p. 38, 64, 95, 174): When placing interior details surrounded by 2–3 outer walls, automatically choose the camera azimuth facing the *open* side of the room (minimizing occlusion rays from existing walls to newly placed interior parts).
72. **[New] Symmetrical Front-3/4 Hero Angle Detection for Vehicles & Buildings** (`75895` p. 55; `10278` p. 1; `42083` p. 1): Detect the model's primary front/facade face (via headlight/door/window/symmetry heuristics or user-set front direction) so Cover, Build Complete, and Hero previews always face the canonical 3/4 front angle.
73. **[New] Auto-Zoom-In on Tiny 1×1 Detail Steps on Large Baseplates** (`10278` p. 19, 38): When placing tiny 1×1 tiles/accessories on a 32×32+ baseplate, either frame tightly around the active quadrant or trigger a floating zoomed inset so 1×1 studs stay >= 12px wide in print.
74. **[New] Dual-Angle Split View for Opposite-Side Simultaneous Placements** (`92176` p. 150; `42083` p. 62): When a step adds parts on *both* front and back faces simultaneously (or requires checking alignment from two sides), support an optional secondary mini-angle inset (e.g., rear view) on the same step slot.
75. **[New] Bottom-Side Assembly Step Auto-Flip Detection** (`10278` p. 214 Step 301): When a step attaches plates strictly underneath an overhanging slab with zero top-surface studs accessible, automatically set `side: 'bottom'` + flip icon instead of rendering hidden pieces from above.
76. **[New] Smooth Camera Azimuth Snapping to Clean 15°/45° Isometric Increments** (`42083` p. 62, 93; `10278` p. 132): Snap auto-selected step cameras to clean canonical angles (`±35°`, `±45°`, `±135°`, `±145°`) so parallel stud rows never suffer ugly near-vertical aliasing artifacts.
77. **[New] Portrait vs. Landscape Aspect-Aware Vertical Cropping for Tall Models** (`92176` p. 85, 150): On tall narrow models (rockets, towers, trees), automatically recommend Portrait orientation in Page Size Diagnostics (or use tall single-column vertical splits in Landscape) to maximize render scale.
78. **[New] Interactive 3D HTML "Reset to Manual Camera" + "Snap to New Parts" Buttons** (`42083` p. 210): In the Interactive 3D HTML viewer, provide one-click camera buttons to zoom directly onto the current step's newly added parts or return to the official manual angle.

## 6. Technic Mechanical Craft — Gears, Axles, Shocks, Orientation Checks & Flexible Parts (14 Findings)
