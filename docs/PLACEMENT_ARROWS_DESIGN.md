# LEGO-Style Placement Arrows & Offsets Design

## Overview

Official LEGO instruction manuals do **not** outline or highlight every newly added piece on every step. Instead, they keep the model clean and readable by offsetting newly added pieces along their physical insertion axis with crisp crimson-red placement arrow(s) whenever:

1. **First step of a build sequence** (2+ parts): keeps the physical base piece(s) grounded and offsets the attached piece(s) along their insertion axes so the builder immediately sees how the initial pieces connect.
2. **Subsequent steps where an added piece could be hard to notice**:
   - **Lateral / horizontal insertion** — pieces attaching to a side face (`±X`/`±Z`) without vertical support underneath (e.g., Technic pins, axles, beams, side brackets, SNOT bricks).
   - **Partial camera occlusion** — pieces tucked behind or inside existing geometry from the step's actual orthographic camera view `(az, el)`.
   - **Color camouflage** — slender or modest pieces placed directly against touching existing pieces of the same or perceptually near-identical color (redmean perceptual RGB distance `< 42`).
   - **Small relative size** — tiny/small details added onto a much larger existing assembly.

Large, contrasting, unobstructed, top-placed bricks on subsequent steps remain seated cleanly with no arrows and no highlight clutter.

## Architecture

- **Core engine**: [`src/engine/placementArrows.ts`](../src/engine/placementArrows.ts)
  - Pure, domain-neutral geometric and perceptual analysis (`computeAutoPlacementOffsetsForSequence`).
  - **Strict single-brick / anti-cluster invariant**: auto placement offsets apply only to isolated individual loose parts and **never** float a multi-brick touching or stacked cluster together as if it were a pre-built subassembly:
    - Contact tolerance is strictly below one plate thickness (`COLOR_NEIGHBOR_EPS = CONTACT_EPS = 6` LDU `< 8` LDU, with `TOUCH_COLOR_EPS = 4` LDU and `SUPPORT_GAP_MAX = 1.5` LDU) so a brick on the far side of an intervening plate never falsely matches as a touching neighbor and horizontal Technic pins sitting ~2 LDU above deck studs are never misclassified as vertically seated.
    - On subsequent steps (`prevParts.length > 0`), only pieces directly contacting `prevParts` (or a same-step grounded host brick for a horizontally embedded pin on imported steps) are eligible, never stacked onto another new piece or supporting a dependent sibling, and broad structural plates (`>= 4×4`) do not trigger slender-piece color camouflage.
    - On both Step 1 and subsequent steps, two physically touching parts are never both offset (checked in both seated and shifted positions), and lifted pieces + arrow corridors are projected into orthographic 2D screen space so nearby offset placements do not crowd or occlude each other.
    - Swept withdrawal corridor checks (`buildSweptCorridorBox`, strict leading-side corridor expansion, and non-cardinal swept sampling at `t = 0.25, 0.5, 0.75`) reject any direction that passes through a blocking sibling part or non-coaxial solid obstacle, returning `null` (keeping the piece cleanly seated) when all directions are blocked instead of forcing a colliding offset. Only verified coaxial host/rod mates (`coaxialHostIds`, matching `coaxialAxis === axis` with deep `> 6.0` LDU overlap along the insertion axis) are exempted from corridor blocking — preventing standard `4.0` LDU stud overlaps between stacked plates from ever bypassing solid-brick collision checks.
  - **Physical stud-normal engagement priority & concave `subBoxes` matching**: [`datCompiler.ts`](../src/engine/datCompiler.ts) extracts exact local positions and outward unit normals for all external studs (top studs and rotated SNOT side studs, excluding underside `stud3`/`stud4` anti-studs) alongside tight convex `subBoxes` for concave parts (such as L-shaped brackets, arches, and frames). [`studioRuntime.ts`](../src/studioRuntime.ts) transforms studs, `localUp` (`[m[1], m[4], m[7]]`), and `subBoxes` into world space. `isStudEngagingPart` and `analyzeContacts` test each convex sub-box so a plate plugging vertically upward into the horizontal top flange of an L-bracket matches the flange's true underside rather than the bracket's deep outer AABB skirt. Whenever a piece attaches onto existing studs (or plugs its own studs into an existing piece), the physical stud normal takes top priority as the sole insertion/offset axis (with zero perpendicular or backward fallbacks).
  - Distinguishes **vertical through-hole & coaxial sleeve insertions** (`±Y`, requiring genuine coaxial XZ containment `≥ 72%` of the narrower footprint on both horizontal axes — including flat round-hole tiles, half-bushes, rings, and Technic plates threaded onto a protruding vertical rod/axle, while ensuring tall vertical SNOT tiles with 4 LDU side-stud overlap are never misclassified as vertical rods, and enforcing closed-top / closed-bottom direction limits `verticalCoaxialTopBlocked` / `verticalCoaxialBotBlocked` so bottom-protruding wheels or underside pins tucked beneath a closed roof never pull upward through solid geometry), **horizontal coaxial insertions** (`±X`/`±Z`, requiring strict vertical containment `nestedInsideRefY` inside the host brick's Y span so stacked 12-LDU-tall plates with 4 LDU top/bottom stud overlap are never misclassified as horizontal Technic pins), and **purely lateral insertions** (`±X`/`±Z`, which lock strictly to `lateralNormal` with zero vertical/through-host fallback so side-mounted wheels or pins on the camera-occluded far side never falsely pull upward through a solid host roof).
  - **Asymmetric headed-shaft direction enforcement (`computeMeshHeadedShaftDir`)**: [`datCompiler.ts`](../src/engine/datCompiler.ts) compares the perpendicular 3D cross-section span of opposite end bands on shaft-like meshes (`L >= 20`, `perpMax <= 22`, `narrow <= 13.5`, `wide >= 1.20 × narrow && wide - narrow >= 2.5` LDU — automatically identifying towball-axles, stop-axles, and studded pins from pure geometry). When a headed shaft inserts coaxially into a hole, `chooseInsertionDirs` locks its withdrawal direction strictly to `headedShaftDir` (with zero opposite-side fallback) so a wide ball/stop is never pushed backwards through a narrow hole.
  - Collision-aware swept-corridor offset resolution (`resolveOffsetAlongAxis`) that targets a crisp ~20 LDU visible arrow gap, enforces a minimum `16` LDU clear air gap past any coaxial corridor obstacle tip (e.g., an axle protruding through a bush or the outer face of a Technic brick hole) with `arrowTo` at the entry tip, automatically selects the open hole end for horizontal coaxial pins, anchors arrows directly over the actual engaged studs or contact overlap (`arrowBox`), and raises vertical lifts via exact orthographic 2D ray-exit sightline clearance (`tFront * tan(|el|) + 20`) so wide overhanging plates never block the camera's view of their attaching studs or arrows.
  - **Orthographic attachment-point & arrow visibility clarity gate (`evaluateOffsetArrowVisibility`)**: Before accepting any offset, casts orthographic rays along the step camera's `viewDir` from the actual rendered arrow shaft and attachment tip (`t = 0.92`) against existing/sibling bricks and the offset piece itself (`shiftedBox`). Rejects offsets that push a piece away from the camera when its attachment point or arrow is obscured behind overhangs/walls (e.g., pushing a cantilevered underside plate downward `-Y` while viewing from above, or pushing a rear SNOT tile deeper behind a tall wall), keeping the piece cleanly seated instead of making the step less clear; meanwhile, when lifting toward the camera behind a foreground wall or when an offset piece blocks its own attachment tip, steps through larger `extra` lift increments so the piece clears into open view.
  - Camera-aware stud-aligned arrow segment generator (`computePlacementArrowSegments`):
    - On the perpendicular stud plane (XZ for vertical lifts, `horizPerpAxis × Y` for lateral/SNOT insertions), aligns arrows directly in the center of the stud **nearest** to the camera and the stud **furthest** from the camera (computed from camera azimuth `azDeg`).
    - For single-stud pieces (`1×1`), emits **1 arrow** centered on that stud.
    - For multi-stud pieces along one axis (`1×N` / `N×1`), emits **2 arrows** centered on the nearest and furthest end studs.
    - For pieces wider than 1 stud in both axes (`M×N`, e.g., `2×2`, `2×4`, `2×6`, `4×4`), emits **2 arrows positioned diagonally** on the nearest corner stud and furthest corner stud relative to the camera.
  - Camera-facing arrow geometry builder (`buildPlacementArrowGeometry`):
    - Defaults to **Flat 2D** (`arrowStyle: 'flat'`), **no outlines** (`arrowOutlines: false`), and **no lighting** (`arrowLighting: false`) — rendered as crisp camera-facing 2D ribbons in a dedicated WebGL pass across `WebGLBrickEngine`, `StudioPhotoRenderer` (unlit depth-tested after SSAO so arrows never cast shadows or receive SSAO darkening), and Interactive 3D HTML Export.
    - All three appearance options (`arrowStyle: 'flat' | '3d'`, `arrowOutlines: boolean`, `arrowLighting: boolean`) are customizable per theme in the Style dialog.
  - Tight clutter caps (`MAX_FIRST_STEP_OFFSETS = 6`, `MAX_SUBSEQUENT_STEP_OFFSETS = 2`, up to `3` for mandatory lateral/coaxial insertions) and strict tiny-piece thresholds (`maxHoriz <= 24 && height <= 18 && vol <= 8500`) so standard contrasting 1×2 bricks stay cleanly seated without cluttering the step.
- **Coaxial Technic pin/axle-to-hole & 3-axis interlocking socket arrows + sub-module attachment anchoring**:
  - `detectCoaxialPinHoleArrows` purely geometrically detects any slender coaxial Technic pin/axle/shaft (`perp <= 16.5` LDU, elongated `>= 1.4×` along the insertion axis) engaging a wrapping hole-bearing host deeper than a stud (`> 4.5` LDU), emitting dedicated `extraArrows` / `extraSegments` along the exact shaft centerline between the protruding tip of the axle/pin and the top/entry face of the receiving hole (in addition to any stud-engaged arrows).
  - `detectInterlockingSocketArrows` detects non-shaft 3-axis interlocking turntable/socket/snap-collar connectors (such as a 2×2 turntable flat base `27448` snapping 4 LDU into a 4×4 round plate opening `60474`, or deeper interlocking sockets) where the connector interlocks without standard `stud.dat` studs along the insertion axis. It emits a **single centered connector arrow** directly from the lifted connector's snap face to the stationary socket's entry center, computes a camera-aware **socket-clearing lift** (`nearOverhang * tan(|el|) + 22`, clamped to `88` LDU) so wide subassemblies (like wheeled bogies or rotating turrets) lift high enough to uncover the entire mating socket plate cleanly — matching official LEGO manual conventions — and skips passive smooth bearing tiles (`smoothTop: true`) and non-connecting outer studs.
  - `computeSubassemblyAttachmentOffsets` refines multi-part sub-module attachment steps (such as pre-built underside modules and wheeled assemblies) using compiled `.dat` studs, coaxial shafts, and interlocking sockets across all sub-module parts so stud arrows anchor strictly to the actual engaged stud region (`arrowBox`, `arrowTo`) rather than an arbitrary centroid brick's full bounding box, while emitting dedicated coaxial or socket `extraArrows` (with `suppressPrimaryArrow: true` when a sub-module attaches solely via coaxial pins or interlocking sockets with zero stud clutch).
  - In [`src/engine/stepCamera.ts`](../src/engine/stepCamera.ts), downward-lifted underside sub-modules that receive a protruding vertical Technic pin/axle into a top-facing hole automatically flip upright (`side: 'top'`, `cue: 'flip_vertical'`) whenever an upright top view clearly reveals both the lifted sub-module and the protruding axle tip — exposing the top-facing hole and pin entry instead of hiding them behind the sub-module's solid underside (while staying `side: 'bottom'` when tucked deep under an occluding hull).
- **Step 1 connection-seam camera orientation**: [`src/engine/stepCamera.ts`](../src/engine/stepCamera.ts)
  - `computeStep1ConnectionFacingCost` scores top views on Step 1 when an upper piece rests on and overhangs a lower piece, preferring a horizontal viewpoint where the lower base sits in front of the upper piece so the attaching studs and connection seam face directly toward the camera (rotating 180° horizontally when the default corner would hide the seam behind the upper overhang).
- **Step generation integration**: [`src/studioRuntime.ts`](../src/studioRuntime.ts)
  - `assignAutoPlacementOffsets(chapters)` runs automatically at the end of `optimizeManualStepCameras(chapters)` so every build sequence (main sections, bags, join sequences, and standalone submodels) uses each step's final camera angle, refining non-section `attachedSubmodel` default offsets via `computeSubassemblyAttachmentOffsets` and computing individual auto offsets for regular steps.
  - Upside-down underside pocket steps enforce strict inverted-gravity layer prerequisites (`aboveMap[i].some(u => pocketRem.has(u))`) so lower underside layers never stack onto upper underside layers within the same step.
  - Stored on `step.autoPartOffsets`, cleanly separated from structural `step.defaultPartOffsets` (e.g., join slide/rod/cap snaps) and user manual `state.overrides[step.id].partOffsets`.
  - Combined via `getBaseDefaultPartOffsets(step)` and `getEffectivePartOffsets(step, ov)` across step framing, live rendering, Parts mode editing/reset/nudging, overview thumbnails, PDF export, and Interactive 3D HTML export.
- **Theme & Rendering Style defaults**: [`src/themes/customThemes.ts`](../src/themes/customThemes.ts) & [`index.html`](../index.html)
  - `highlightNew` ("Outline New Parts") defaults to `false` across all built-in themes and remains available as an opt-in theme toggle.
  - `autoPlacementArrows` ("Placement Arrows & Offsets") defaults to `true`, `arrowColor` defaults to `#dc2626` (official LEGO crimson red), `arrowStyle` defaults to `'flat'` (2D), `arrowOutlines` defaults to `false`, and `arrowLighting` defaults to `false` — all editable in the Style dialog and persisted on themes.
- **Interactive 3D HTML Export**: [`src/export/interactiveHtmlExporter.ts`](../src/export/interactiveHtmlExporter.ts)
  - Step scenes carry camera-aware `arrowSpecs` (plus fallback `arrows`), `arrowColor`, `arrowStyle`, `arrowOutlines`, and `arrowLighting`, rendered dynamically in WebGL as the user orbits the 3D scene.
